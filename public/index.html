05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Can you fix my computer remotely?+

Many Windows, email and software issues can be investigated remotely if your computer turns on and has a working internet connection. Tell me what’s happening first so I can check whether remote help is suitable.

What happens during remote support?+

After we agree on an appointment, I’ll guide you through starting a temporary support session. You approve access, can watch the screen and can end the connection. Unattended access isn’t kept by default.

Do I need to give you my password?+

Please don’t send passwords through this website or by message. You should enter your own passwords whenever possible. If a sign-in is needed, we’ll discuss it during the appointment.

What if you can’t fix the problem?+

Not every issue can be fixed in a session. I’ll explain what I’ve found and any options for a next step. We’ll agree on the charges before starting; time spent investigating may still be billable. There’s no automatic “no fix, no fee” promise.

What kinds of devices do you support?+

The focus is Windows laptops and desktops, printers and scanners, plus basic home Wi-Fi troubleshooting. If you have a different device, ask first. Support for other operating systems isn’t assumed.

Do you repair broken screens or hardware?+

I focus on setup and troubleshooting. Broken screens, liquid damage, internal component repairs and similar work need a hardware repair specialist. Advanced security incidents also need specialist help.

Can you recover deleted files?+

I can help check ordinary places such as the Recycle Bin or an existing backup where appropriate. I don’t offer advanced data recovery. If important files are missing or a drive may be failing, stop using it and contact a data-recovery specialist.

Do you support company-owned computers?+

This service is for personal and home technology. For a company-managed device, contact your employer’s IT team. I won’t bypass their access rules, security settings or account restrictions.

Where is in-person support available?+

In Hamilton, by appointment. We’ll confirm the location, travel suitability and scope before arranging a visit. There’s no walk-in repair shop.

How and when do I pay?+

Payment is generally due after the service by Interac e-Transfer. Cash is also accepted for appropriate in-person appointments. I’ll confirm pricing before work starts and ask before adding time or doing more complex work. This site doesn’t collect card information.

Can I ask about my problem before booking?+

Yes. Use the quick inquiry form above to describe the problem. Sending an inquiry doesn’t book an appointment or commit you to paid work.

When are you available?+

Support is by appointment. Share a preferred date and time window, and I’ll reply to confirm what’s possible. The request form doesn’t show live availability or reserve a slot.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?+

Please let me know as soon as you can by replying to your appointment email. Repeated no-shows may require prepayment before another appointment is booked.
