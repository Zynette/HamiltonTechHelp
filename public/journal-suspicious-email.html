Recognize suspicious emails

Published November 24, 2026

Pause when a message pressures you to act quickly.

Check the full sender address, even when the display name looks familiar.

Reach the organization through its app or a website you already know.

Avoid unexpected attachments and sign-in links.

A polished message can still be fraudulent.

If you shared account access, use the provider's official recovery process and tell the affected organization.
