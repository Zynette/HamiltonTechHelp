WEBSITE CARE — QUOTED BY SCOPE

Keep your website current, without an open-ended contract.

Need a text change, updated service information, repaired link or mobile layout improvement? I can review a defined update request remotely and quote it before work begins.

Ongoing website care is a proposed service, not an active subscription. Recurring hours, response windows, renewals and cancellation terms need written owner approval before offering a plan.

01 / ONE-TIME UPDATES

One-time updates

  • Text, pricing, photos and link updates where account access is authorized
  • A review of an affected mobile layout or broken inquiry form
  • Backup or change-approval steps appropriate to the site's platform
02 / POTENTIAL RECURRING CARE

Potential recurring care

  • Agree a monthly edit limit or hours rather than unlimited requests
  • Define response window, supported website platform and exclusions in writing
  • Clarify rollover or non-rollover, cancellation and third-party fees before any recurring billing
03 / NOT A 24/7 MANAGED SERVICE

Not a 24/7 managed service

  • No continuous uptime monitoring, emergency repair guarantee or unlimited redesigns
  • No platform or plugin subscription is included by default
  • Complex migrations, incidents and custom development require separate review

NO AUTOMATIC BOOKINGS OR PAYMENTS

A written scope before we start.

All work is remote and subject to availability. Any price, taxes, revisions, timeline, third-party charges and payment arrangements are confirmed before an agreement.
