Your information.

Names, email addresses, optional phone numbers, device details, appointment preferences and website project briefs or URLs you submit are used to respond, assess suitability, prepare quotes, arrange work and maintain basic service or payment records where needed. An inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing messages.

Share only what’s needed

Don’t send passwords, banking credentials, credit card information, your SIN, government-login details or other sensitive credentials. There are no file uploads or payment-card fields on this site. If Stripe invoicing is connected later, billing details and payment status will be handled through Stripe’s hosted tools; this site will not store card numbers.

Handling your details

Your information isn’t sold. It is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the service, follow-up and required records. You can ask about access, corrections or deletion by contacting techspecialistsupport@gmail.com; some records may need to be retained.

When configured, forms use a server-side Formspree relay to the receiving inbox. A request is not sent if the provider is unavailable. Formspree and the receiving email provider may process information outside Canada. Quotes and payment instructions are coordinated manually after a request; no payment service is connected to these forms. The website does not keep a separate customer database or log form contents.

A quieter website

No advertising trackers or analytics are installed. Fonts are served from this website. Temporary technical information is used to reduce repeated spam requests. Appearance preferences are saved only in your browser. If you continue from the Tech Clinic, selected answers are held in temporary tab storage until the inquiry is filled. Hamilton weather is requested by our server; your precise location is not requested.

Terms for tech help and websites.

These plain-language terms explain how remote tech help and website projects work. They apply after we agree in writing on scope, price and the next step. An inquiry is not a confirmed booking, and not every problem can be fixed.

Scope, estimates and payment

I’ll describe the proposed work and price before starting. Prices are in CAD; applicable taxes, third-party charges and payment timing will be identified before they are added. A defined task keeps its quoted price if it finishes early. New work or a material scope change needs your approval.

Remote sessions and availability

Tech support is quoted from $40 for an agreed task or diagnostic appointment. The written quote states the scope, expected duration and price. A diagnostic appointment is an assessment and does not guarantee a repair. I work remotely only, by appointment and subject to availability. Messages aren’t monitored continuously; emergency or 24/7 support is not provided.

Website projects

Website work is quoted individually after reviewing requirements. Before work starts, we agree in writing on scope, page count, revision allowance, timeline, price, payment stages and handover, including files, account ownership and access. Hosting, domain registrations, paid tools and any ongoing maintenance are clearly accounted for in the quote. They are not included by default. Scope changes require agreement on any added cost and time.

You must have permission to use supplied text, images and other materials. We agree on a backup and approval process before changes to an existing site. Larger stores, custom applications, advanced integrations and urgent ongoing maintenance are reviewed individually. There is no promise of flawless results, guaranteed search rankings, unlimited revisions or continuous support. Any maintenance must be separately agreed.

Your responsibilities

You confirm that you own or are authorized to access the device and accounts involved. Back up important files before a session and tell me which files matter. Do not send passwords, banking details or other sensitive credentials through this website.

Remote support and changes

Remote sessions are temporary and customer-controlled. You approve the connection, can watch what happens and can end it. I won’t bypass passwords, account-ownership checks or company security controls. Proposed updates, installations and settings changes will be explained before significant work.

Limits and specialist referrals

Physical device repairs are not offered. I don’t open phones, tablets, laptops or desktops, replace screens or batteries, or repair internal components or liquid damage. Those jobs need a qualified hardware repair provider. Advanced data recovery, serious security incidents and company-managed devices may also need a specialist. I do not promise a particular outcome or guarantee that a problem will be fixed. If a drive may be failing, stop using it and seek specialist advice.

Deposits, cancellations and rescheduling

The written quote will state whether a deposit is required and the applicable cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms before any payment. Website project changes or cancellations are reviewed against agreed scope and completed work. These are draft service terms subject to owner and legal review. Nothing here removes rights or legal exceptions that cannot be excluded.

Fair limits

To the extent permitted by law, responsibility is limited to the agreed service and fees paid for that service. Nothing here removes consumer rights or other protections that cannot legally be excluded. If an important issue is unclear, ask before work begins.

Payment setup and accessibility

Payment requests, if any, are arranged manually after a written quote is accepted. There is no connected card processor, automatic invoice, subscription or calendar on this website. Any invoice will state the service scope, amount and due date.

Accessibility and questions

If you need an accessible way to review these terms or use the site, contact techspecialistsupport@gmail.com. Questions about a service or these terms can be sent there before requesting work.
